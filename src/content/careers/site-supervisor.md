## About the role

We're looking for an experienced Site Supervisor to lead crews across our residential and
commercial projects, keeping work on schedule, on budget, and to the highest standard.

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate trades and daily site activity
- Enforce health-and-safety standards
- Track progress against the project schedule
- Liaise with the project manager and clients

### Requirements

- 5+ years in construction, 2+ in a supervisory role
- Strong knowledge of building codes and safety regulations
- Excellent communication and leadership skills
