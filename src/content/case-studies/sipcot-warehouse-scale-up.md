## The brief

A third-party logistics operator had signed a lease with a national retail chain,
committing to a fixed handover date for a large-format distribution warehouse at SIPCOT,
Chennai. The lease carried financial penalties for late delivery — construction timeline
was no longer just a schedule, it was a contractual obligation.

## The challenge

At 14 months, the program was tight for the scope involved: structural steel fabrication
and erection, long-span PEB roofing, high-load RCC flooring, and full site civil works,
including dock infrastructure for an 18-bay yard. Any single delayed trade — steel
fabrication, roofing, or flooring cure times — risked cascading into the lease date.

## Our approach

We ran structural steel fabrication off-site in parallel with foundation and civil works
on-site, so erection could begin the moment footings were ready rather than waiting on a
sequential start. Roofing followed directly behind steel erection, bay by bay, so weather
protection was in place early and interior trades could start without waiting for full
building closure.

### Scope of work

- Structural steel fabrication and erection
- PEB roofing and wall cladding
- RCC flooring rated for high-bay racking loads
- 18-bay dock yard, fire exits, and site civil works

## The result

The building was handed over on the contracted date, with zero lost-time safety incidents
across the full program. The client's racking contractor began installation the following
day — the fastest handover-to-occupancy turnaround we've delivered on a project this size.
