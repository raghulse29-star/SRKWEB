## The brief

A local government body needed six blocks of a school campus re-roofed after years of
monsoon leaks had damaged classroom ceilings. The only possible construction window was
the school's term break — and the reopening date was fixed. There was no flexibility to
extend into term time with students on campus.

## The challenge

Re-roofing six separate blocks — including safe removal of aging asbestos sheeting under
regulatory disposal requirements — is normally sequenced block by block. Done that way,
this project would have run well past the reopening date.

## Our approach

Instead of a sequential block-by-block approach, we split the crew across all six blocks
in parallel from day one, with a dedicated asbestos-removal team working ahead of the
roofing crews. Ceiling repair and repainting followed immediately behind roofing on each
block, rather than waiting for all six roofs to finish first.

### Scope of work

- Safe removal and regulated disposal of damaged asbestos sheeting
- New corrugated metal roofing across all six blocks
- Ceiling repair and repainting in affected classrooms
- Gutter and downpipe renewal to prevent future water pooling

## The result

All six blocks were re-roofed, repaired, and repainted in three weeks — inside the term
break, with the school reopening on schedule and no classrooms out of use.
