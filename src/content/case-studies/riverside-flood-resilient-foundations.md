## The brief

A local developer had secured a riverside plot near Srirangam for a 24-unit residential
complex — a strong location for buyers, but one with a documented history of seasonal
flooding from the Kaveri. Getting the foundation design wrong here wasn't a minor risk; it
was the difference between a saleable asset and a liability.

## The challenge

Standard slab-on-grade foundations common on inland sites weren't appropriate this close to
the river. We needed a foundation and site-drainage strategy that accounted for the
highest recorded water levels at the site, not just typical-year conditions — while still
keeping the project commercially viable for the developer.

## Our approach

We raised the building plinth above the site's documented flood line and designed a
dedicated perimeter drainage system to route seasonal water away from the foundations
rather than relying on natural runoff. Roofing and terrace waterproofing were specified to
the same standard, since heavy seasonal rainfall was as much a factor as river flooding.

### Scope of work

- Raised-plinth, flood-resilient foundation design
- RCC framed structure across four floors
- Dedicated perimeter site drainage
- Weatherproof terrace roofing and waterproofing across all units

## The result

The complex was delivered across three phases, with all 24 units handed over ready for
fit-out. Through two subsequent monsoon seasons — including one with river levels near the
historical flood mark — no water-related defects have been reported.
