## The brief

A developer planning a three-block commercial office park directly on Chennai's East Coast
Road came to us with a specific worry: they'd seen other coastal buildings in the area
develop visible corrosion on exposed steel within a few years of completion. They wanted
the building specified correctly from day one, not repaired later.

## The challenge

Standard mild-steel fixings and untreated structural sections degrade quickly under
constant salt-laden air — rust streaks on facades, weakened roof fixings, and shortened
service life on anything exposed to the elements. Retrofitting corrosion protection after
the fact is expensive and never as effective as specifying it correctly at the outset.

## Our approach

We specified corrosion-resistant structural steel grades and marine-rated fasteners across
every exposed connection, not just the visible facade. Roofing and cladding systems were
selected specifically for coastal-rated performance, and detailing at joints and
penetrations — the points where corrosion typically starts — was reviewed line by line
before fabrication began.

### Scope of work

- Corrosion-resistant structural steel and fixings throughout
- Salt-air-rated roofing and cladding systems
- Basement parking and civil works across three blocks
- Landscaped courtyards and pedestrian walkways

## The result

The finished park carries a 10-year corrosion warranty on all exposed steelwork — a
commitment we were only able to make because the material choices were right from the
start, not patched in afterward. Two years in, the facade shows no early corrosion signs
typical of standard-spec coastal buildings.
