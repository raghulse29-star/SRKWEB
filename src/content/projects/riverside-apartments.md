## Project overview

A local developer engaged SKR to deliver a 24-unit residential complex on a riverside plot,
with strict requirements around flood-resilient foundations and weatherproof roofing given
the site's proximity to the Kaveri.

### Scope

- Raised-plinth flood-resilient foundation design
- RCC framed structure across four floors
- Weatherproof terrace roofing and waterproofing
- Common-area civil works, compound wall, and landscaping

Delivered on schedule across three phases, with all 24 units handed over ready for fit-out.
