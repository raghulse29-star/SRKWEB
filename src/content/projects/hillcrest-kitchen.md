## Project overview

We removed a load-bearing wall to open the kitchen into the living area, then rebuilt the
space with custom cabinetry, new flooring, and updated lighting.

### Scope

- Structural opening with steel beam
- Custom cabinetry and stone countertops
- New flooring and lighting
- Plumbing and electrical updates

The result is a bright, open space that has become the heart of the home.
