## Project overview

A boutique resort on the Yercaud hills needed its guest cottages re-roofed ahead of the
monsoon, with materials that could handle sustained heavy rainfall and cooler hill
temperatures without compromising the rustic aesthetic guests expect.

### Scope

- Weatherproof sloped roofing across 14 guest cottages
- Timber-look finish over a modern insulated roof deck
- Rainwater channeling and drainage redesign
- Night-time work scheduling to avoid disrupting guests

Delivered ahead of the monsoon deadline, with the resort remaining fully operational throughout.
