## Project overview

A growing firm needed two floors fitted out while the rest of the building stayed
occupied. We phased the work to minimize disruption and kept the common areas open
throughout.

### Scope

- Partition and structural alterations
- New HVAC and electrical
- Custom joinery and finishes
- Phased delivery around live operations

The fit-out was handed over ready for move-in, with zero days of business interruption.
