## Project overview

The owners of this 1990s independent villa wanted an open, modern layout without a full
rebuild. We opened up the ground floor, replaced the aging RCC terrace waterproofing, and
gave the facade a contemporary refresh.

### Scope

- Non-load-bearing wall removal to open the ground floor
- Terrace waterproofing and slope correction
- Modernized facade with new cladding and lighting
- Full electrical and plumbing rewiring

Completed in ten weeks while the family remained on-site, with work sequenced floor by floor.
