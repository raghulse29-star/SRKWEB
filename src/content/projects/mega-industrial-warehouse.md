## Project overview

A third-party logistics operator needed a large-format warehouse shell erected on a tight
schedule to meet an incoming lease deadline. SKR handled the structural steel fabrication,
long-span roofing, and civil works as a single turnkey package.

### Scope

- Structural steel fabrication and erection
- PEB roofing and wall cladding
- RCC flooring rated for high-bay racking loads
- Dock levelers, fire exits, and site civil works

Handed over on a 14-month program, on schedule, with zero lost-time safety incidents.
