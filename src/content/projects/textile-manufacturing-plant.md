## Project overview

An expanding textile mill needed additional roofed floor area without interrupting
production in the adjoining units. We phased erection around live machinery and delivered
a thermally insulated roof system built for the heat and humidity of a working mill floor.

### Scope

- Precision roofing over an active production facility
- Thermal insulation and ventilation detailing
- Phased erection to avoid disrupting operations
- Skylights and natural-light panels to cut daytime lighting load

Delivered with the mill running throughout, and zero production downtime attributed to the works.
