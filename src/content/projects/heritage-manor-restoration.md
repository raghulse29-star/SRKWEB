## Project overview

This 100-year-old colonial bungalow's original wooden truss roof had suffered years of
water ingress and structural decay. The family wanted the leaks and rot solved without
losing the Madras-tile silhouette that gives the house its character.

### Scope

- Structural assessment and selective truss reinforcement
- Lightweight modern roofing system built to mimic the original tile profile
- Full waterproofing and gutter renewal
- Sensitive restoration of eaves and heritage timber detailing
