## Project overview

A three-block commercial park on the East Coast Road needed materials and detailing rated
for constant salt-air exposure. We specified corrosion-resistant roofing and structural
fixings throughout to protect the long-term investment.

### Scope

- Corrosion-resistant structural steel and fixings
- Salt-air-rated roofing and cladding systems
- Basement parking and civil works across three blocks
- Landscaped courtyards and pedestrian walkways

Completed on a 16-month program, with a 10-year corrosion warranty on all exposed steelwork.
