## Project overview

Years of monsoon leaks had damaged classroom ceilings across this government school's six
blocks. The local body needed the work completed within the term break, before students
returned.

### Scope

- Removal of damaged asbestos sheeting, safely disposed per regulation
- New corrugated metal roofing across all six blocks
- Ceiling repair and repainting in affected classrooms
- Gutter and downpipe renewal to prevent future water pooling

Completed in three weeks flat, ahead of the school reopening.
