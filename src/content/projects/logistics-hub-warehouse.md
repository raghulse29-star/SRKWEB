## Project overview

A national logistics client needed a distribution hub built to support high-bay racking
and heavy daily truck turnover, on a plot near the Sriperumbudur industrial corridor.

### Scope

- Pre-engineered steel structure sized for high-bay racking
- Heavy-duty RCC flooring rated for reach-truck loads
- 18-bay dock yard with levelers and canopy
- Fire hydrant network and compliance sign-off

Handed over in 11 months, fully compliant with fire and safety norms from day one.
