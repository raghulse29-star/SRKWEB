Choosing a new roof is one of the bigger decisions you'll make as a homeowner. The right
material protects your home for decades; the wrong one can mean repairs and headaches.

## Asphalt shingles

The most common choice — affordable, widely available, and easy to repair. Expect 15–25
years of life depending on quality and climate.

## Metal roofing

A higher upfront cost, but metal roofs last 40–70 years, shed snow and rain well, and are
highly energy efficient. A strong fit for harsh-weather regions.

## Tile and slate

Beautiful and extremely long-lasting, but heavy — your structure may need reinforcement.
Best suited to certain architectural styles and climates.

## The bottom line

Match the material to your climate, how long you plan to stay, and your budget. When in
doubt, a free inspection and quote will tell you exactly what your home needs.
