Small roof problems become expensive ones fast. Watch for these five signs and call for
an inspection if you notice any of them.

## 1. Water stains on ceilings

Brown rings or damp patches usually mean water is getting past the roof somewhere above.

## 2. Missing or curling shingles

Shingles that are cracked, curling, or gone leave the underlayment exposed.

## 3. Granules in the gutters

Excess shingle granules washing into your gutters signal an aging roof surface.

## 4. Sagging areas

A visibly sagging roofline can indicate trapped moisture or structural issues — don't wait.

## 5. A spike in energy bills

A failing roof lets conditioned air escape, pushing heating and cooling costs up.

Spotting any of these? A quick inspection now is far cheaper than a major repair later.
